# Photodrop — Google Play Store Listing Draft

## App Name

Photodrop

## Short Description (80 characters max)

Share photos privately with the people who matter most. Simple, warm, and safe.

## Full Description (4000 characters max)

Photodrop is a cosy, private space for sharing photos with the people you care about.

No public feeds. No algorithms. No likes or follower counts. Just your photos, shared with your group — whether that's family, close friends, a holiday crew, or your new parents' group.

Here's how it works: someone creates a group and invites the people who belong. Everyone drops their photos in, and everyone gets to see them. That's it. Simple as it should be.

📸 Drop your photos
Upload from your camera roll or snap something new. Your photos land in the group feed, ready for everyone to enjoy.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Made for small groups
Photodrop is built for the people you'd actually share a photo album with. Family reunions, weekend trips, everyday moments — keep them all in one place.

🔒 Private by design
Your photos are shared only with the people in your group. No strangers, no public profiles, no data mining.

🔔 Stay in the loop
Get a friendly nudge when someone drops new photos, so you never miss a moment.

🎨 Make it yours
Pick a profile colour that feels like you. It's a small touch, but it makes things feel a bit more personal.

✉️ No passwords to remember
Sign in with a magic link sent to your email. No passwords, no friction, no fuss.

Photodrop was built because the best photos — the real ones, the funny ones, the ones that make you feel something — deserve a better home than a cluttered group chat or a social media feed full of strangers. They deserve somewhere warm, simple, and private.

Download Photodrop and start sharing the moments that matter.

## Data Safety Form

### Data collected

| Data type | Collected | Shared with third parties | Purpose | Optional |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Email address** | Yes | No | Account management (magic link authentication) | No (required to sign in) |
| **Photos** | Yes | No | App functionality (sharing within groups) | Yes (core feature, but user chooses what to upload) |
| **Push notification tokens** | Yes | No | App functionality (new photo notifications) | Yes (user can decline notification permission) |
| **Profile colour preference** | Yes | No | App functionality / personalisation | Yes |

### Security practices

- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
- Data can be deleted upon request (account deletion)
- Data is not sold to third parties

## Privacy Policy Outline

A minimal privacy policy for Photodrop should cover:

1. **What data is collected**
   - Email address (for authentication via magic links)
   - Photos uploaded by the user
   - Push notification device tokens (if permission granted)
   - Profile colour preference
   - Basic usage/server logs (IP address, timestamps)

2. **How data is used**
   - Email: to send magic link sign-in emails
   - Photos: stored and displayed to members of the user's group(s)
   - Push tokens: to send notifications about new photos
   - Profile colour: displayed alongside the user's name in the app

3. **Data sharing**
   - Photos are shared only with members of the same group
   - No data is sold or shared with third-party advertisers
   - Service providers (hosting, email delivery) may process data on our behalf

4. **Data storage and security**
   - Where data is stored (e.g., Cloudflare R2/D1, region)
   - Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
   - Access controls

5. **Data retention and deletion**
   - How long data is kept
   - How users can request account/data deletion
   - What happens to photos when a user leaves a group

6. **Children's privacy**
   - The app is not directed at children under 13
   - No knowing collection of data from children

7. **Changes to the policy**
   - Users will be notified of material changes

8. **Contact information**
   - Email address for privacy enquiries
