# Google Play Store Listing

Copy-paste reference for the Play Console. Privacy policy is already live at `/privacy.html`.

## Store listing

**App name:** Photodrop

**Short description** (80 char max):
> Share photos privately with the people who matter most.

**Full description:**
> Photodrop is a cosy, private space for sharing photos with the people you care about.
>
> No public feeds. No algorithms. No likes or follower counts. Just your photos, shared with your group — whether that's family, close friends, or your new parents' group.
>
> **Made for small groups**
> Photodrop is built for the people you'd actually share a photo album with. Family reunions, weekend trips, everyday moments — keep them all in one place.
>
> **Private by design**
> Your photos are shared only with the people in your group. No strangers, no public profiles, no data mining, no AI training.
>
> **Stay in the loop**
> Get a friendly nudge when someone drops new photos, so you never miss a moment.
>
> **No passwords to remember**
> Sign in with a magic link sent to your email. No passwords, no friction, no fuss.
>
> Download Photodrop and start sharing the moments that matter.

**Category:** Photography
