Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 February 2026

Photodrop is a private photo-sharing app. We collect only what's needed to make the app work and nothing more.

What we collect

  • Email address — used for authentication via magic links. We don't send marketing emails.
  • Photos — uploaded by group admins and stored in Cloudflare R2. Photos are only visible to members of the same group.
  • Push notification tokens — if you enable notifications, we store a device token to deliver photo alerts.
  • Profile colour preference — your chosen avatar colour, stored with your account.

What we don't do

  • No tracking or analytics
  • No third-party advertising
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties

Where data is stored

All data is stored securely using trusted third-party infrastructure providers. Data may be distributed across multiple regions to ensure reliability and performance.

Server logs

Our hosting infrastructure may collect basic server logs including IP addresses and timestamps. These are used solely for security monitoring and debugging, and are not linked to your account.

Data retention

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. Group admins can delete photos at any time. If you'd like your account removed, contact us and we'll delete it.

Children's privacy

Photodrop is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with their data, please contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us through the developer email listed on our Google Play Store page.
