Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 February 2026

Photodrop is a private photo-sharing app. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, where it is processed, and your choices.

Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, profile color, account creation timestamp, and last-seen timestamp.
  • Group data: group name, membership, role (admin/member), join timestamp, and image-protection preference.
  • Photo data: uploaded photos and thumbnails (stored in Cloudflare R2), captions, uploader ID, and upload timestamps.
  • Interaction data: comments, emoji reactions, and photo view timestamps.
  • Authentication and security data: magic-link tokens (with status and expiry fields), refresh-token cookie, and short-lived access/session tokens.
  • Notification data: web push subscription data (endpoint and keys), native push device tokens (iOS/Android), and related registration timestamps.
  • Anti-abuse data: rate-limit records keyed by user ID, email, and/or IP address.

How we use data

  • To authenticate users and keep accounts secure.
  • To provide core features (private group photo sharing, comments, reactions, and member management).
  • To send notifications when new photos are shared (if enabled).
  • To prevent abuse, spam, and unauthorized access.
  • To debug reliability and security issues.

Who can see your data in the app

  • Group members can see photos, captions, comments, reactions, and member names within their group.
  • Group admins can also see member email addresses for their group to manage invitations and membership.

Data processors and infrastructure

  • Cloudflare (Workers, D1, and R2) to run the app and store data/files.
  • Resend to send login and invite emails.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) and platform push providers (Apple/Google) for mobile push delivery.

What we do not do

  • No tracking or analytics.
  • No third-party advertising.
  • No selling of personal data.

Where data is stored

Application data is processed using Cloudflare services and may be stored or cached in multiple regions for reliability and performance.

Device storage

On your device, Photodrop stores local app data including an access token, app preferences (such as theme and install-prompt state), and push-related local state. A secure, HTTP-only refresh-token cookie is also used for session renewal.

Server logs

Our infrastructure and app logs may include IP address, timestamps, request metadata, and error details. We use this information for security, abuse prevention, and debugging.

Data retention

Account and group data are retained while your account is active. Group admins can delete photos. Some technical and security records (for example token and rate-limit records) are retained based on operational and security needs. If you want your account data deleted, contact us.

Children's privacy

Photodrop is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with their data, please contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us through the developer email listed on our Google Play Store page.
